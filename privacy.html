Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 7, 2026

At THANKSES, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data you share with us. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, process, and secure information across our platform modules.

1. Information We Collect

1.1

User Identity Data: Full name, phone number, email address, and country, collected during account creation and phone verification workflows.

1.2

Geolocation Data: Precise GPS coordinates used exclusively to execute mathematical radius queries to populate the "Nearby Services" map layout.

1.3

Recipient/Provider Data: Commercial bank details, IFSC codes, organizational affiliations, and profile photos required to set up verified disbursement channels.

2. How We Use and Share Information

2.1

Transaction Processing: Your payment information is securely shared directly with our payment gateway partner (Razorpay). THANKSES does not store raw credit card numbers or banking passwords on its servers.

2.2

Controlled Identity Transmission: If a user checks the "Thankser (Private)" toggle on checkout, their visible name and avatar are completely redacted from the public feed and recipient transaction history. However, raw metadata remains accessible to system administrators to satisfy standard tax and compliance reporting rules.

3. Data Retention and Security

3.1

Transaction histories and earned digital badges are preserved as long as an account is active to populate the profile metrics correctly.

3.2

All data streams flowing between the FlutterFlow application frontends, database architectures (Firebase/Supabase), and payment gateways are locked behind industry-standard Secure Socket Layer (SSL) encryption protocols.

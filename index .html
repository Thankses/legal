Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: October 7, 2026

Thank you for utilizing THANKSES to support and recognize local service providers. Because our platform deals in instant digital micro-transactions, our refund protocol is structured strictly as follows:

1. Instant Consumption Rule

1.1

All monetary "Thanks" (including Prime, Apex, and Pinnacle Badges) represent instant digital service assets. Once a user confirms a transaction, the Appreciation Badge is generated instantly, the 10% Platform Facilitation Share is deducted, and the remaining 90% is split and released instantly to the worker's ledger via automated routing rails.

1.2
Because these performance rewards are fully consumed and distributed the exact second the payment clears, all monetary transactions executed on the THANKSES platform are strictly final and non-refundable.

2. Exceptions for Technical Errors and Frauds

2.1

Double-Deduction: If a network failure causes your bank account to be debited twice for a single Badge purchase, our backend systems will detect the orphaned transaction. The secondary duplicate payment will be automatically canceled and reversed back to your original payment source within 5 to 7 business days in accordance with standard bank clearing schedules.

2.2

Unauthorized Fraudulent Card Use: If an international card or local UPI asset is used fraudulently on our web application portal, the cardholder must log a formal dispute with their issuing bank. Once verified by our gateway partner (Razorpay), we will freeze the target recipient's wallet balance and cooperate fully to return the compromised funds to the rightful owner.

3. Cancellation of Creator Accounts

3.1

Service providers or managing organizations may deactivate their THANKSES profile boards at any time via their settings portal. Any remaining, unpaid balances held in their wallet at the time of official closure will be pushed through a final settlement cycle to their linked current account, subject to standard 10% platform share deduction rules.
